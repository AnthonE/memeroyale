# Credits

Meme Royale is a fork of [Kong Royale](https://github.com/MaximilianHansen/kongroyale), made by Max Blade and its
contributors, with a lot of help from AI agents, under the MIT licence (LICENSE keeps their notice). The game, its
code and its own art are theirs; the changes here are the reskin.

## Not shipped here

Kong Royale's community work belongs to its creators and was licensed to Kong Royale alone, so this fork leaves it out:
KONG FM 99.8, the tuk-tuk radio (Witzner), and the Toon UI's KONG / ROYALE wordmarks and shield hit icons (RET). The
Kong Royale name and logo are not reused either.

## Triple T

The player, Triple T, is the Gates and oneshot character, commissioned from Meshy and owned outright by the Elo Pros
operator. He is **not covered by the MIT licence**: play and modify the game with him, but ask before reusing him
anywhere else. Files: `public/assets/actors/tt/`, `tools/actors/src/triple-t.glb`.

## The guns

The guns, the rocket and the frag (RATIO BOMB) are oneshot's, commissioned from Meshy and owned outright by the Elo
Pros operator. Like Triple T they are **not covered by the MIT licence**. Files: `public/assets/weapons/`.

## The announcer

The announcer's voice, Kallixis, was recorded with ElevenLabs on the Elo Pros operator's paid plan: fifteen lines by
`tools/audio/vo.mjs`, the rest taken from oneshot's announcer. Like Triple T, it is **not covered by the MIT licence**.
Files: `public/assets/audio/kr/vo/`.
